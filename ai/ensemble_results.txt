  How it was reached: PCA and LSTM scores were each z-scored against their own
  TRAIN score distribution, averaged, then smoothed with a backward 10-sample
  rolling mean whose threshold is taken from the smoothed TRAIN scores. No step
  uses a test label, a test score or a future sample, and the headline variant
  (average + smoothing) is fixed in advance, not picked as the best-on-test.
  Best honest PR-AUC on this file: 0.75. Ensembling + smoothing lifted the
  headline well above PCA alone (0.56), but on this stand-in it lands around
  the seed-averaged LSTM (0.75) and did NOT reach ~0.85. The weak PCA partner
  caps how much the average can gain. So on this data the temporal model is already
  close to the ceiling, and honest ensembling does not beat it by much. I would rather
  report a real 0.75 than tune anything on the test labels to reach 0.85.
  These are numbers on the SYNTHETIC stand-in file (swat_like.csv), not real data.
  Real ICS data is much harder: on the real HAI dataset the same pipeline scores
  PR-AUC roughly 0.05-0.28 (see hai_detector.py). Read this table as a check that
  the method and the leakage guards work end to end, not as a real-world score.
